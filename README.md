## Game Story  

Astro’s world was reduced to ashes by a corrupt regime that sought only destruction. From the ruins, he rose—gathering outcasts, the betrayed, and the forgotten—to strike back. Branded a fugitive, he became a symbol of resistance, sabotaging warships and exposing the truth, igniting doubt among the people. With each victory, the corrupt’s grip weakened, but the final battle loomed—the Iron Tyrant, where the architects of destruction awaited.  

> **Alone, he was a fugitive.**  
> **With his crew, he was an army.**  
> **And when the stars burned again, they would burn for his vengeance.**  

**Astro Avengers:** Fight alone. Fight together. But never forget—some battles are personal.

- 🎮 **Interactive Gameplay** – Easy-to-follow game mechanics with smooth controls.

- 🛡️ **Power-ups** – Gain shields, health, and other boosts to aid your mission.  

---

## How to Play  

1. **Multiplayer Mode (Experimental)**  
   - Create a lobby by clicking on the **add (+) icon**.  
   - Invite your friends and form your **League of Villains** to raid the enemy.  
   - If the multiplayer mode is unstable, continue as a **single-player** in the lobby.  

2. **Single-Player Mode**  
   - If multiplayer does not work, create a lobby and click **Start** to play solo.  
   - Choose your **villain character** and **spaceship** to begin your journey.
